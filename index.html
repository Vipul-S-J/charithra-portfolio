Selected work
01 / About

It’s still the same thing.
Just more intentional.

This portfolio begins with a childhood memory: loving makeup, bright clothes, getting dressed, and the quiet excitement of being seen.

Today, that instinct has become an intentional practice of composition, styling, textile exploration and visual storytelling.

The work moves between resort wear, hand-flat knitting, styling, embroidery, draping and print design.
