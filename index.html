01 / ABOUT

It’s still the same thing.
Just a little more intentional now.

This portfolio begins with a childhood memory: loving makeup, bright clothes, getting dressed, and the quiet excitement of being seen. Today, that instinct has become a more intentional practice of composition, styling, textile exploration and visual storytelling.

The work moves between resort wear, hand-flat knitting, styling, embroidery, draping and print design — with memory, identity and materiality as recurring threads.

03STYLING PROJECT
03 — STYLING

A style crossover built around contradictions: sport and shimmer, discipline and excess, jerseys and curated accessories. A collision of wardrobes where habits blur and genres tangle.

04EMBROIDERY PROJECT
04 — EMBROIDERY

An embroidery project rooted in childhood summers, family memories and the quiet world of Malgudi Days. Each motif holds a fragment of stories, warmth and everyday life.

05DRAPING PROJECT
05 — DRAPING

A gown built around a siren who does not chase attention — she disrupts it. Drapes appear almost collapsed while retaining control, balancing chaos and precision.
